Who we are

Students helping students. In every community.

Student Helping Organization of Malawi (SHOAM) is a student-led organisation working with students in villages, towns and schools across the country. We are students, parents, teachers and volunteers who believe that education is for all — and that help should arrive before a child leaves school.

We share books and notebooks, honest information about school costs and choices, and the kind of practical support that turns “I wish someone had told me” into “here is what you can do next”.

Education is for all. That sentence is only worth printing if we are willing to do the work behind it.

Where the focus is

The journey starts early. The barriers start early too.

SHOAM is built for Malawian students and the families behind them — the young people weighing school fees, uniforms, transport, books, distance and the question of what comes next after school.

We keep the younger-student photos because that is where the journey begins. College and university remain a path for some; our job is to make sure no student is lost on the way there.

What drives us

A clear mission, a stubborn vision

Not a slogan on a wall — the test we hold ourselves to every school term.

01

Our mission

To help students across Malawi stay in school, get the materials and information they need, and find a local community that takes their ambitions seriously.

02

Our vision

A Malawi where education really is for all — where a student's future is not decided by their village, their family income or the cost of getting to school.

03

Our values

  • Education for all — the motto is the point
  • Student-led — Malawian students are the organisers
  • Local and nationwide — villages, towns and schools
  • Fees, books, transport and guidance before speeches
  • Honest about what is still being built
