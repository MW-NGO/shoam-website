Why we write

If we don't tell our story, someone else will.

Development language turns people into beneficiaries. We would rather be students, teachers, neighbours and friends — with names, opinions and a sense of humour.

So we write it down. The exam that nearly did not happen. The meeting that changed our mind. The volunteer who noticed a student had stopped coming. Stories are evidence, too.
