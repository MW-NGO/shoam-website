Being a member

What we ask of each other

Not a contract. A culture.

Show up

If you say you will be there, be there. Reliability is the first leadership skill and the hardest to fake.

Protect people

Safeguarding is everyone's job. We take concerns seriously, follow up, and never trade someone's safety for a good photo.

Keep records

Minutes, budgets, attendance, consent. Written down so the work can be repeated and trusted.

Leave the door open

Make it easier for the next person than it was for you. That is how a small team becomes a movement.
