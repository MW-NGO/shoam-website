Before you write

Questions we get often

If your question is not here, ask it. We would rather answer than guess.

Can I join if I do not live in Malawi?

Yes. Some of our members study or work elsewhere and contribute remotely. Be clear about what you can do from where you are — translation, design, research, mentoring online, or support during holidays.

Do I have to pay to become a member?

No. Membership is not a fee. It is a commitment of time and conduct. If any activity has a real cost (for example transport to a residential training), we will say so in advance and look for support first.

How is money handled?

We are building this carefully, so please message us before sending money. We will confirm the current account with a named member, explain what the support will be used for, and tell you how it will be recorded. We do not publish payment details that could be copied or misused.

I am a student with an idea. Will you fund it?

Not yet — our Education for All Fund is still being designed. In the meantime, write to us anyway. We can help you shape the idea, find a mentor, or point you to someone doing similar work.

Can you guarantee a reply?

We will reply to every genuine message, but not instantly. If you have not heard within a week, send a short follow-up on Messenger — that is helpful, not rude.

Do you work with schools and colleges?

Yes. Local schools and teachers are at the centre of the work. We also welcome colleges, universities and student groups who want a session on further study, materials, mentoring or staying on track. Please use the subject “School or campus visit”.
